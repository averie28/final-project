Norway National Football Team

The Norway team placed 5th place out of 48 teams in the 2026 World Cup.
For 28 years the Norway National Football Team was not a participant in the FIFA World Cup until this year.
Erling Haaland is the best striker on the Norway team and is considered one of the best strikers in the world. He has scored 36 goals in 36 games for Norway.
