History of Norway

Norway became an independent kingdom in 1905 after peacefully separating from Sweden.
The Viking Age shaped early Norwegian culture, trade, and exploration across Europe.
Norway was occupied by Germany during World War II, and the resistance played a major role in the country’s history.

Viking History Facts

  • Vikings lived in Scandinavia from about the late 8th century to the 11th century.
  • They were skilled sailors who explored, traded, and raided across Europe and beyond.
  • Viking warriors often used longships that could travel quickly on rivers and open sea.
  • They established settlements in places such as Iceland, Greenland, and North America.
  • The Viking Age helped shape the culture and history of modern Norway.
